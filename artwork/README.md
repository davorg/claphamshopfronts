# Clapham Shopfronts watercolours

Created with the built-in imagegen tool from the eight project photographs in `pics/`. These are decorative, AI-generated interpretations for the holding page; the photographs remain the historical source material.

The hero collage is composed in HTML and CSS so the pictures rearrange for small screens. Each full-size WebP is 1448 × 1086 pixels; the `-small.webp` versions are 640 × 480 and are selected through responsive `srcset` attributes.

A [flattened collage](../docs/images/hero-collage.webp) is also saved at 1200 × 630 pixels for reuse and link previews. It was exported from the finished HTML/CSS composition in Chromium.

## Final prompts

Each generation used its corresponding original photograph as the sole referenced image. The exact prompts follow.

### street-sign

```text
Use case: style-transfer.
Asset type: individual watercolour painting for a Clapham Shopfronts village-hall noticeboard collage.
Input image: the attached original photograph is the edit target and factual source.
Primary request: Turn this photograph of the Clapham High Street SW4 street plaque and its brick wall into a charming, genuinely hand-painted pastel watercolour on warm ivory cold-pressed watercolour paper. Keep the actual London architecture and shopfront proportions recognisable. Faithfully retain the major shop signs and their spelling, shutters, graffiti, scaffolding, and everyday urban character wherever present. The affectionate incongruity of a delicate English village watercolour of this very urban Clapham shop is intentional.
Style: loose translucent washes, subtle pigment blooms, visible dry-brush edges, fine irregular warm-grey pencil detail, restrained dusty rose, pale ochre, sage, powder blue and lilac. Light and luminous, with softly feathered painted edges meeting unpainted paper. Consistent series suitable for a local history exhibition.
Composition: landscape 4:3 painting, centre the whole main subject, reduce excess road and sky to bring the shopfront close while retaining the distinctive upper architectural details; for the street plaque retain the complete sign. Small blank ivory margin on all four edges. No external captions, no new shops, no flowers, no countryside, no people, no artist signature, no decorative tape or pins, no frames, no watermarks. This must look painted, not a photograph with a filter.
```
