Clapham Shopfronts

Documenting the changing shopfronts of Clapham, London.

About the Project

Clapham Shopfronts is an ongoing project to photograph and document the businesses on the streets of Clapham and how they change over time.

Shops open, close, merge, split, and change use. Over time, this project aims to build a photographic and historical record of those changes — a small archive of a London high street in transition.

What We’re Recording

  • New business openings
  • Shop closures
  • Vacant units
  • Refurbishments
  • Before and after comparisons
  • How individual shop units change over time
